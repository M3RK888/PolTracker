How this estimate works

Individual House races are rarely polled, so every district is estimated the same way: its partisan lean (Cook PVI) applied to the national environment from the generic congressional ballot, currently . A district rated R+5 is about 5 points more Republican than the country in two-party share, roughly 10 points of margin, so with the nation at D+8 it comes out near D-2.

Cook's 2026 PVI is calculated for the district lines actually used this year, so states that redrew their maps mid-decade (Texas, California, Missouri, North Carolina, Ohio, Utah and others) are covered.

This ignores candidates: incumbents, scandals and money often move a district several points from its lean. Ratings use the same cutoffs as the other maps. A snapshot of the polls, not a forecast.
