Reading this table

Margin is the Democrat's share minus the Republican's, in points. D+3 means the Democrat leads by 3.

Weight is how much a poll counts. A fresh 600-person poll scores about 1.0. Weight halves every days, rises with the square root of sample size, and is reduced when one pollster has several polls of the same race. Share is that poll's slice of its average: the national average for national polls, or that state's estimate for state polls (the rest of a state's estimate comes from its 2024 baseline).

Adjusted applies to state polls only: the margin shifted by how far the national average has moved since the poll was taken.

Status. "Too old" polls ended more than days ago and no longer count. "No national poll" means the poll is recent but its matchup has no national poll in that window, so there's no baseline to blend it with yet.

Polls collected by hand from public releases and aggregators; each row links to its source.
